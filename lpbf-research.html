Research Project

Multivariate Modeling of Powder Spreading in Laser Powder Bed Fusion

Project Overview

This project investigated how particle properties and processing parameters influence powder-bed quality in laser powder bed fusion.

My Contributions

I designed and conducted more than 40 GPU-accelerated discrete element method simulations, analyzed the resulting powder-bed data, and developed a simulation framework representing multiple spreading conditions.

Methods

The work combined discrete element method simulations, design of experiments, statistical modeling, and powder-bed quality analysis.

Outcome

The study supported a first-author publication in Powder Technology and improved understanding of process–structure relationships in LPBF.
